International Journal Of Informative & Futuristic Research (IJIFR)

Digital Nudging: An Analysis Of E-Commerce UI/UX In Shaping Green Consumer Decisions

A S Ashwin, Reshmi A Rajan, Deepak Thothadri J, Anantika RB

International Journal Of Informative & Futuristic Research (IJIFR), Volume 13, Issue 03, November 2025.

This research explores how UI/UX interventions and behavioral economics drive sustainable consumer choices in e-commerce. Despite a clear intention action gap where users desire to support the environment but often refuse to pay premium prices strategic design can effectively bridge the divide. The study reveals that setting eco-friendly options as the default choice (driving 38% of decisions), leveraging peer influence (impacting 88% of users), and implementing personal environmental impact tracking are highly effective UI/UX strategies for promoting sustainable online shopping habits.
