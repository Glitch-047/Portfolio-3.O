How I Work

Synthesised from Big Five + RIASEC + Career Discovery assessments

🎯

Goal-driven & Structured

Very high Conscientiousness means I work best with clear objectives, structured timelines, and a strong sense of accountability. I finish what I start.

🔍

Analytical before Action

My top RIASEC type is Investigative — I prefer to understand a problem deeply before acting. I'm comfortable with ambiguity but resolve it through data and reasoning.

🗣️

Assertive Communicator

High Assertiveness (19/20) within moderate Extraversion — I speak up, take charge in discussions, and lead confidently, while also knowing when to listen.

🧘

Calm Under Pressure

Low Neuroticism scores indicate exceptional emotional regulation. I tend to stay composed and clear-thinking in stressful or high-stakes situations.

💡

Creative & Open to Ideas

High Openness (77) means I genuinely enjoy new approaches, creative problem-solving, and intellectual curiosity — not just incremental thinking.

🤝

People-Aware, Not People-Pleasing

Moderate-high Agreeableness with low Modesty — I'm cooperative and empathetic, but I'll disagree when the situation calls for it and stand by my position.

All assessments on this page were taken voluntarily in August 2026 for self-reflection and career exploration purposes. Scores represent self-reported responses and should be interpreted as one data point among many, not as definitive or clinical measurements.
