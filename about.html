My Background

Rooted in Finance,
Branching into Management

I began my professional journey in corporate accounting and finance — a world defined by precision, accountability, and meticulous attention to detail. Working in that environment gave me a solid foundation in how organisations actually function beneath the surface: cash flows, compliance, ledgers, and the quiet discipline of getting numbers right.

My undergraduate degree in B.Com (Professional Accounting) at Kumaraguru College sharpened both my technical skills and my understanding of business fundamentals. But the more I learned, the more I wanted to zoom out — to understand strategy, leadership, and the human side of organisations.

The Transition

From the Balance Sheet
to the Bigger Picture

"Finance taught me to read a business. Management is teaching me to lead one."

Moving into postgraduate management studies was a deliberate choice. I wanted to take the analytical rigour I'd built in finance and combine it with a broader set of leadership, strategy, and organisational skills. The transition wasn't a departure — it was an evolution.

Today I sit at an interesting intersection: I can understand a P&L as fluently as I can facilitate a team discussion. I think that cross-disciplinary perspective is one of my genuine strengths, and it's something I continue to develop.
